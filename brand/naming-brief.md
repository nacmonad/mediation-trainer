# Naming Brief

## Assignment

Name the standalone master brand formerly called **Mediation Trainer**. **Obversa** was selected on 2026-08-30; trademark clearance remains pending.

The product lets mediators rehearse difficult conversations with independently configured simulated Parties, then review the Session. It begins as a closed-alpha tool for solo mediators and mediation trainers, with room to expand into trainer workflows, authored Scenarios, and adjacent high-stakes conversation rehearsal.

## Desired character

- Evocative rather than descriptive
- Language-neutral and able to travel across English and French at minimum
- Calm, serious, tactile, trustworthy, and precise
- Compact, ideally two or three syllables with one obvious pronunciation
- Credible as a professional practice instrument, not a legal-tech database, therapy app, game, course provider, or generic AI wrapper

## Competitive calibre

The name should support product polish and memorability comparable to Yoodli and Mursion, with the professional seriousness associated with Harvard PON. It must not imply institutional authority, enterprise scale, or validated outcomes that the closed alpha has not earned.

See [competitive-landscape.md](research/competitive-landscape.md) for the first-party market scan.

## Semantic territories

Prioritize names grounded in:

1. Preparation before entering a consequential room
2. Independent perspectives held in productive relation
3. Orientation and judgment under uncertainty
4. A bounded encounter or deliberate rehearsal craft

## Constraints

- Standalone brand, not part of an existing brand family
- Avoid `mediation`, `mediator`, `AI`, `coach`, `trainer`, or `simulation` in the master name
- Avoid faux Latin/legal roots, institutional containers, combat language, gamification, and synthetic tech suffixes
- Do not imply guaranteed neutrality, agreement, settlement, prediction, or a single correct outcome
- Exact `.com` is preferred but not mandatory; a credible prefix domain or appropriate alternative TLD is acceptable
- Domain and GitHub identity are mandatory screening concerns
- npm and major social handles are useful but may use scoped or qualified variants
- The current repository does not need to be renamed until a finalist is approved

## Availability gate

Every presented finalist must receive:

- Web and same-category prior-art searches
- GitHub repository and organization checks
- Domain checks, including practical variants
- npm/package-name checks
- Initial Canadian, US, and international trademark screening
- English/French pronunciation and adverse-meaning review

No candidate becomes final without professional trademark review.
