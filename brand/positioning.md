# Obversa Positioning

**Status:** Working positioning for closed alpha  
**Date:** 2026-08-30

## 01. Category definition

### Stated category

AI-assisted mediation training software.

### Actual category

When practitioners decide whether to use Obversa, they compare it with human role-play, static training Scenarios, generic AI chat, communication-coaching products, and doing no structured rehearsal at all.

### Opportunity category

**Mediation rehearsal.**

This is narrower and more ownable than communication coaching, more active than mediation education, and more professionally credible than AI role-play. It names the behavior Obversa enables without making the model provider or technology the category.

### Recommendation

Lead with **mediation rehearsal**. Use **professional simulation for mediators** as a supporting descriptor when more explanation is needed. Keep “AI-assisted” in product details and proof, not at the front of the brand.

## 02. Competitive landscape

The useful map has two axes:

- **Institution-led to practitioner-controlled**
- **Instruction/content to active rehearsal**

### Competitor positions

- **Harvard PON:** institution-led, spanning instruction and facilitated role-play. Owns scholarly authority.
- **Mediator Academy:** accessible instruction with a clear category label. Owns global mediation education.
- **Yoodli:** product-native, self-directed communication practice. Owns judgment-free speaking practice.
- **Mursion:** enterprise-led, active rehearsal for difficult workplace conversations. Owns high-stakes simulation at organizational scale.
- **Bodyswaps:** accessible, immersive perspective practice. Owns embodied role exchange.
- **Second Nature:** enterprise rehearsal tied to sales execution. Owns repetition until commercial behavior becomes automatic.
- **Attensi:** enterprise simulation tied to gamified, measurable behavior change.

### Obversa position

Obversa sits at the practitioner-controlled, active-rehearsal edge of the market. It is purpose-built for mediation process rather than general communication, sales, VR, or broad enterprise learning.

### Open territory

**Calm, practitioner-controlled rehearsal for mediations where each Party has a genuinely separate perspective, private information, and limits.**

## 03. Positioning territory

### The space

Obversa is the private rehearsal room for mediation judgment.

### The audience owned

Obversa is the obvious choice for practicing mediators and mediation trainers who want repeatable rehearsal without coordinating human role players.

### The competitive moat

Obversa does not simulate one agreeable chatbot wearing two names. Each Party is independently configured with private facts, behavioral state, negotiation constraints, and an exact-audience view of the Session. A deterministic mediation engine controls process mechanics while practitioners retain judgment about what to do and say.

The open-source, self-hostable foundation makes the product inspectable and extensible. The moat is not merely access to a language model; it is the domain model, authored Scenarios, participant separation, procedural boundaries, and practitioner trust built around them.

Over time, the moat compounds through an evidence-backed Evaluator, researched and practitioner-authored Scenarios, private and curated public libraries, document-preparation workflows, and community feedback. These are product horizons, not closed-alpha claims.

## 04. Positioning statements

### Standard

For practicing mediators and mediation trainers, Obversa is the mediation rehearsal platform that provides repeatable practice with independently simulated Parties, because each Party maintains its own private facts, constraints, behavior, and view of the Session.

### Strategic, internal

Obversa creates a new mediation-rehearsal category between static professional education and generic AI role-play, owning practitioner-controlled simulation with independent Parties and disciplined procedural boundaries.

### Human, public-facing

Practice the difficult mediation before you enter the room, with Parties that have their own facts, limits, and point of view.

## 05. Reasons to believe

1. **Independent Parties:** Party A and Party B can use different providers, models, personas, private facts, and behavioral profiles.
2. **Real process boundaries:** Joint Session, private Caucus, Agreement, impasse, and Walkout are explicit parts of the simulation.
3. **Exact-audience views:** Every Event declares who may observe it; each Party receives only its authorized Projection.
4. **Deterministic domain rules:** Plain TypeScript controls negotiation state, Offers, disclosure, thresholds, and outcomes rather than delegating authority to model improvisation.
5. **Inspectable foundation:** The application is open source under AGPL-3.0-only and supports local/self-hosted use.
6. **Disciplined alpha boundary:** Closed-alpha Scenarios and resources are synthetic; the product does not invite confidential client material.

### Future proof system

The following will strengthen the position after validation, but must not be marketed as current capability:

1. **Evidence-backed review:** A separate Evaluator grounds findings in the completed Session Event log and professional standards.
2. **Credible Scenario research:** Available cases and source material inform carefully researched exercises rather than shallow generated prompts.
3. **Scenario creation and ownership:** Practitioners can author, validate, keep private, or submit Scenarios for curated public use.
4. **Local document preparation:** `presidio-web` supports redaction and preparation before material enters the Scenario workflow, subject to a separate privacy and threat-model review.
5. **Community knowledge:** Reviews, ratings, feedback, attribution, curation, and moderation help useful Scenarios improve over time.

## 06. What Obversa refuses to be

- Obversa is not legal advice, mediation services, outcome prediction, or a substitute for professional judgment.
- Obversa is not a generic chatbot that agrees with the user or plays every role from one undifferentiated context.
- Obversa does not score mediation as a game with one winning script.
- Obversa will not build trust on vague “safe AI” claims while hiding how Party context and Session boundaries work.
- The closed alpha is not for real confidential case files, privileged material, sensitive personal information, or minors' data.

## 07. Positioning in one sentence

Obversa gives mediators a private place to rehearse difficult Sessions with independent simulated Parties before entering the real room.

## Five-year ambition

Become the trusted practice environment for mediation and other high-stakes conversations: prepare source material, author or choose credible Scenarios, rehearse, receive evidence-backed review, and improve through practitioner knowledge.
