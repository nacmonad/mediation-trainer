# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Obversa (selected working brand; trademark clearance pending)
- **Category**: Mediation rehearsal; professional simulation for mediators
- **Description**: Rehearse difficult mediations with independent AI Parties, then grow from evidence-backed review into a trusted Scenario and practitioner-learning platform.
- **Stage**: Working MVP preparing for a closed alpha
- **Website**: https://obversa.org (registered 2026-08-30; site rollout pending)

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Solo mediators and mediation trainers; later, small dispute-resolution and legal teams
- **Key Problem**: Practitioners need repeatable, realistic rehearsal without coordinating human role players or exposing confidential client material.
- **Their Language**: mediation, mediator, parties, joint session, caucus, scenario, case resources, offer, agreement, impasse, walkout, session review

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Two independently configured AI Parties maintain private facts, behavioral state, negotiation constraints, and exact-audience views inside a deterministic mediation simulation engine. The project pairs transparent self-hosting with a future managed service.
- **Competitors**: Direct and aspirational references include Harvard Program on Negotiation, Mediator Academy, Yoodli, Mursion, Bodyswaps, Second Nature, and Attensi. Practical alternatives remain human role-play, generic AI chat, and static mediation exercises.
- **Market Position**: The practitioner-controlled, active-rehearsal edge of the market: purpose-built mediation practice with independent Parties and disciplined process boundaries.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: calm, serious, tactile, trustworthy, precise
- **Tone**: Professional and human; calm rather than clinical, serious without becoming legalistic
- **Voice Admires**: To be determined

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: confidentiality boundaries, realistic practice, practitioner agency, transparency, disciplined simulation
- **Mission**: Help mediators rehearse difficult conversations safely and repeatedly so they enter the room better prepared.

## Product Horizon
- **Launch Wedge**: Closed-alpha mediation rehearsal using synthetic Scenarios and independent simulated Parties.
- **Evaluation**: A separate Evaluator will review completed Sessions and produce evidence-backed findings about the Mediator's conduct; it will not participate in the live Session or reduce mediation to one winning script.
- **Scenario System**: Visual Scenario authoring, private Scenario libraries, curated public publishing, and research workflows for developing credible exercises from available cases and source material.
- **Document Preparation**: A future `presidio-web` pipeline will support local redaction and preparation before documents become Scenario material; it is not a current confidentiality guarantee.
- **Community Layer**: Practitioner feedback, reviews, ratings, attribution, curation, and moderation around public Scenarios.
- **Platform Arc**: Prepare materials, author or select a Scenario, rehearse, evaluate, and improve through accumulated practitioner knowledge.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Validate activation, completed Sessions, repeat use, and demand for reusable custom Scenarios through a constrained closed alpha using synthetic material only.
- **Key Metrics**: invite activation, Session completion, seven-day repeat use, provider reliability, qualitative practitioner feedback, requests for custom Scenario creation
