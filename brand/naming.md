# Naming Finalists

**Status:** Obversa selected; trademark clearance pending  
**Date:** 2026-08-30

The naming process began with three territories: threshold/orientation, independent perspectives, and rehearsal craft. Rehearsal language produced useful messaging but no honest master-brand survivor after prior-art screening. The final slate therefore favors threshold and perspective metaphors.

## Selection

### Obversa

Selected by the founder on 2026-08-30.

The brand will carry the idea that every difficult conversation has more than one face. The product lets a Mediator rehearse with independent Parties and constraints without collapsing them into one compliant chatbot or promising agreement.

Repository, package, and public-surface renaming are intentionally deferred until clearance and rollout planning.

## Evaluated finalists

### 1. Sojlo

**Pronunciation:** SOY-lo  
**Origin:** Esperanto for a threshold, sill, or doorstep.

**Story:** The product is the threshold before the consequential room: a bounded place to rehearse before crossing into the real encounter.

**Why it works:**

- Five letters and two syllables
- Calm, tactile, and drawable
- Coined-looking to English and French audiences without using a synthetic technology suffix
- Broad enough to support mediator practice, trainer tools, Scenarios, and adjacent difficult-conversation rehearsal
- Carries the strongest version of the market whitespace: "the room before the room"

**Descriptor tests:**

- Sojlo | Mediation rehearsal
- Sojlo | Practice before the room
- Rehearse the conversation with Sojlo

**Availability snapshot:**

- Exact `sojlo.com`: registered, with no meaningful operating product found in the initial scan
- `sojlo.io`: appears available
- `sojlo.ai`: appears available
- `getsojlo.com`: appears available
- `usesojlo.com`: appears available
- GitHub `sojlo`: appears available
- npm `sojlo`: appears available
- Initial exact-name web searches found no active software, AI, training, or mediation product
- Initial Canadian, US, and WIPO-indexed exact-name searches surfaced no mark; professional clearance is still required

**Risk:** The Esperanto pronunciation is not self-evident from spelling. Some people may initially guess SOZH-lo. This is manageable but must be tested aloud with practitioners.

**Working score:** 88/100

## Other finalist

### 2. Obversa — selected

**Pronunciation:** ob-VER-sa  
**Origin:** From *obverse*: the face turned toward the observer, paired conceptually with another face.

**Story:** Every difficult conversation has more than one face. The simulator lets a mediator work with independent accounts and constraints without collapsing them into one compliant chatbot.

**Why it works:**

- Three syllables with a serious, composed sound
- Encodes perspective without promising agreement or neutrality
- More semantically legible after one sentence than a purely invented name
- Extensible beyond mediation

**Descriptor tests:**

- Obversa | Mediation rehearsal
- Obversa | Practice across perspectives
- Run the Scenario in Obversa

**Availability snapshot:**

- `obversa.org`: secured by the founder on 2026-08-30; selected canonical domain
- Exact `obversa.com` and `obversa.ai`: registered
- `obversa.io`: appears available
- `getobversa.com`: appears available
- `useobversa.com`: appears available
- Exact GitHub handle: occupied; a qualified organization would be required
- npm `obversa`: appears available
- No operating same-name software or training product surfaced; nearby `Observa` and `Obvera` names increase phonetic noise
- Initial Canadian, US, and WIPO-indexed exact-name searches surfaced no mark; professional clearance is still required

**Risk:** The meaning is obscure, the construction can feel faintly Latinate, and it is close in sound to existing `Observa`/`Obvera` technology names.

**Working score:** 79/100

## Reserve finalist

### 3. Chiasma

**Pronunciation:** kee-AZ-ma or kye-AZ-ma  
**Origin:** A crossing where distinct lines meet and change relation without losing their identity.

**Story:** Two independent Party trajectories cross inside a bounded Session, while the Mediator practices how to work with the relationship between them.

**Why it works:**

- Rich, specific perspective metaphor
- Visually distinctive and capable of supporting a strong mark
- Does not imply harmony, settlement, or a single correct answer

**Availability snapshot:**

- Exact `chiasma.com` and `chiasma.ai`: registered
- `chiasma.io`: appears available
- `getchiasma.com`: appears available
- `usechiasma.com`: appears available
- Exact GitHub handle: occupied; a qualified organization would be required
- npm `chiasma`: appears available
- Prior art exists in biotech, academic publishing, a cloud-platform module, and facilitation methodology; no direct mediation-rehearsal product surfaced
- Initial Canadian, US, and WIPO-indexed exact-name searches surfaced no mark, but the broader prior art makes professional clearance especially important

**Risk:** Two accepted pronunciations, medical/biological associations, and nontrivial unrelated prior art. Advance only if the sound strongly resonates with users.

**Working score:** 72/100

## Names deliberately rejected

Strong-sounding candidates were eliminated because active adjacent products already use them, including Anteroom, Parvis, Prova, Practa, Repeto, Turnwise, CuePoint, Roomtone, Tacet, Pilotage, Temper, Bearings, Crosswise, Partwise, Thirdroom, Prelude, Tablework, and Offbook.

## Decision guidance

**Obversa** is the selected working brand. Its perspective metaphor was preferred over Sojlo's threshold metaphor despite the weaker namespace position. Before public rollout, the phonetic proximity to `Observa`/`Obvera` and the relevant software, SaaS, and training classes need a professional knockout search.

Do not rename the repository or purchase brand assets on this document alone. First:

1. Say each finalist aloud to five mediators or trainers without showing the spelling.
2. Show the spelling and ask for pronunciation and recall five minutes later.
3. Obtain a Canadian trademark lawyer's knockout search for the preferred name in the relevant software, SaaS, and education/training classes.
4. Secure the remaining core namespaces before announcing it. The canonical `obversa.org` domain is already secured.
